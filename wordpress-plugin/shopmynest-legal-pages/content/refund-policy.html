Return & Refund Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

ShopMyNest is a marketplace for handmade and one-of-a-kind goods. Every order is fulfilled by an independent Seller, and returns are handled directly between the Buyer and the Seller. This policy explains the framework that applies to every sale on ShopMyNest, how disputes are resolved when the Buyer and Seller can't reach agreement, and the narrow circumstances in which ShopMyNest will step in and refund from the Seller's balance.

The short version

  • Contact the Seller first. Every refund or return conversation starts with a direct message to the Seller through the order in the app.
  • ShopMyNest mediates only if you can't reach a resolution. If you and the Seller can't agree, ShopMyNest will step in as a neutral mediator.
  • ShopMyNest reserves the right to refund from the Seller's balance when a purchase is significantly not as described (SNAD) or fraudulent, even without the Seller's consent.
  • Because items are handmade and often one-of-a-kind, ShopMyNest does not offer "changed my mind" returns by default. Individual Sellers may choose to accept them.

Step 1 — Contact the Seller

Open the order in the ShopMyNest app and tap Message seller. Explain the issue with clear photos of the item and the packaging (especially if damaged in transit). Most issues are resolved directly between the Buyer and the Seller within a few messages: a partial refund, a replacement, a return shipping label, or an exchange.

Sellers are expected to respond within 3 business days. If a Seller doesn't respond within that window, or if you and the Seller cannot agree on a resolution, move to Step 2.

Step 2 — Ask ShopMyNest to mediate

If the direct conversation with the Seller doesn't produce a resolution, email [CONTACT EMAIL] with your order number, a short description of the issue, the photos you shared with the Seller, and a copy of the message thread. A ShopMyNest administrator will review both sides and propose a resolution: a full refund, a partial refund, a return with a prepaid label, or no refund. Our decision is binding on both the Buyer and the Seller for the purposes of the ShopMyNest platform.

Step 3 — ShopMyNest's right to refund from the Seller

ShopMyNest reserves the right to issue a refund to the Buyer from the Seller's balance, without the Seller's consent, when the purchase is significantly not as described (SNAD) or fraudulent. The bar for this action is high; ShopMyNest exercises it sparingly and only after reviewing evidence from both sides. Examples that meet this bar include:

  • Item never shipped, tracking never scanned, and the Seller is unreachable.
  • Item received is materially different from the listing — wrong item, wrong color, wrong size, missing components, counterfeit, or misrepresented condition.
  • Item was represented as one thing (e.g. handmade) and is clearly another (e.g. mass-produced or dropshipped).
  • Evidence of a fraudulent listing, stolen photos, or a Seller impersonating another maker.

Small variations that are normal for handmade goods — grain, weave, finish, minor color shifts under different light — are not "not as described" and do not meet this bar.

What doesn't qualify for a ShopMyNest-issued refund

  • Buyer's-remorse returns, unless the Seller offers them (their listing will say so).
  • Custom, personalized, or made-to-order items once production has started, unless the item is defective or not-as-described.
  • Items damaged after delivery.
  • Items returned outside the return window or in a materially different condition from how they were delivered.
  • Digital goods, once delivered.
  • Perishable goods.

Return window

You have 7 days from delivery to raise an issue with the Seller. If the Seller doesn't respond within 3 business days, you have 7 additional days to escalate to ShopMyNest mediation. Requests raised after these windows may be declined.

Evidence you should include

  • Clear photos of the item as received, from multiple angles.
  • Photos of the packaging (especially if damaged in transit) and the shipping label.
  • Screenshots of the listing at the time of purchase, if the issue is about the description.
  • The full message thread with the Seller.

Who pays return shipping

  • If the return is because of Seller error, damage in transit, or a defect, the Seller pays return shipping. The Seller will send a prepaid label through the app or reimburse the Buyer for a label they buy.
  • If the Seller offers voluntary returns for "changed my mind" and the Buyer is using that option, the Buyer pays return shipping unless the Seller's listing says otherwise.
  • If ShopMyNest mediates and rules in the Buyer's favor for SNAD or fraud, return shipping (if any is required) is charged to the Seller's balance along with the refund.

Don't ship an item back without authorization

Don't return an item until either the Seller or ShopMyNest has confirmed the return in writing. ShopMyNest cannot guarantee a refund for items shipped back on the Buyer's own initiative without a return authorization.

When refunds are issued

Refunds are issued to the Buyer's original payment method through Stripe and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on the Buyer's bank. Refunds issued from the Seller's balance are deducted immediately from the Seller's available funds; if the available balance is insufficient, ShopMyNest may deduct from future earnings until the refund is fully covered.

Partial refunds

ShopMyNest may offer or approve a partial refund when the item is broadly as described but has minor issues that don't justify a full return — for example, a small cosmetic defect the Buyer and Seller agree to price down instead of ship back.

Chargebacks

Open a ShopMyNest refund request before filing a chargeback with your bank. Chargebacks lock the order and prevent us from refunding you directly, which usually slows resolution and can result in the Seller being charged additional fees by their card processor. If you've already filed one, email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will coordinate.

Order cancellations

  • Before the Seller ships. You can request a cancellation in the app; if the Seller hasn't shipped yet, they'll approve and we'll refund your original payment method.
  • After the Seller ships. Cancellation isn't possible; treat it as a return once the item arrives.
  • Sellers may cancel an order if the item can no longer be made or has been damaged before shipping. In that case ShopMyNest refunds the Buyer in full.

Shipping label refunds

ShopMyNest purchases carrier shipping labels through Shippo on the Seller's behalf as soon as the Buyer's payment clears. A purchased label can be refunded and the postage credited back only if both of the following are true:

  1. The label has not been used — meaning the carrier has not scanned the package into the mailstream; and
  2. Fewer than 14 days have passed since the label was purchased.

Once either condition is no longer met, the label is final and the postage cost is non-refundable. This 14-day window is set by the carrier through Shippo and cannot be extended by ShopMyNest. When an order is cancelled or refunded before the item ships, ShopMyNest automatically requests the label void with Shippo and, if the void is accepted, credits the postage back to the Seller's payout ledger. If the carrier declines the void — most commonly because the label has already been scanned or the 14-day window has closed — the postage is not refunded and remains payable.

Seller-specific policies

Sellers may offer more generous return terms than this policy (for example, 30-day buyer's-remorse returns). They may not offer less generous terms. If a Seller's listing conflicts with this policy, this policy wins.
