Return & Refund Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

ShopMyNest is a marketplace for handmade and one-of-a-kind goods. Each order is fulfilled by an independent Seller, and returns are ultimately handled by the Seller. This policy explains the default framework that applies to every sale on ShopMyNest and what to do when something goes wrong.

The short version

  • If your order arrives damaged, defective, significantly not-as-described, or never shows up — you're eligible for a refund.
  • You have 7 days from delivery to open a return request in the app.
  • Because items are handmade and often one-of-a-kind, we don't offer "changed my mind" returns by default. Individual Sellers may choose to accept them; if they do, it's stated on the listing.

What qualifies for a refund

You're eligible for a refund when:

  1. Damaged in transit. The item arrived broken or clearly damaged.
  2. Defective. The item doesn't function as advertised or has a clear defect that wasn't disclosed in the listing.
  3. Significantly not as described. The item is materially different from the listing photos or description (wrong item, wrong size, wrong color scheme, missing components, etc.). Small variations that are normal for handmade goods — grain, weave, finish, minor color shifts under different light — are not "not as described."
  4. Never delivered. Tracking shows the order didn't arrive and the Seller can't confirm delivery within a reasonable time.

What doesn't qualify

  • Buyer's-remorse returns, unless the Seller offers them (their listing will say so).
  • Custom, personalized, or made-to-order items, once production has started, unless the item is defective or not-as-described.
  • Items damaged after delivery.
  • Items returned outside the return window or in a materially different condition from how they were delivered.
  • Digital goods, once delivered.
  • Perishable goods.

How to request a refund

  1. Message the Seller first. Open the order in the app and use the built-in messaging. Most issues get resolved quickly, one-to-one.
  2. Open a formal return request within 7 days of delivery if the Seller doesn't respond within 3 business days or if you can't agree on a resolution.
  3. Provide evidence. Clear photos of the item, the packaging (especially if damaged in transit), and the shipping label help us resolve quickly.
  4. Ship the item back only when the Seller or ShopMyNest confirms. Don't return items without a return authorization — we can't guarantee a refund for items shipped back on your own.

Who pays return shipping

  • If the return is because of Seller error, damage in transit, or a defect, the Seller pays return shipping. The Seller will send a prepaid label through the app or reimburse you for a label you buy.
  • If the Seller offers voluntary returns for "changed my mind" and you're using that option, the Buyer pays return shipping unless the Seller's listing says otherwise.

When refunds are issued

Once the returned item is delivered back to the Seller (or once damage/loss is confirmed and no return is needed), we refund your original payment method through Stripe. Refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

If the Seller doesn't respond within 5 business days of the return being delivered, ShopMyNest may issue the refund from the Seller's balance on your behalf.

Partial refunds

We may offer a partial refund when the item is broadly as described but has minor issues that don't justify a full return — for example, a small cosmetic defect you and the Seller agree to price down instead of ship back.

Chargebacks

Please open a ShopMyNest return before filing a chargeback with your bank. Chargebacks lock the order and prevent us from refunding you directly, which usually slows things down. If you've already filed one, email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll coordinate.

Order cancellations

  • Before the Seller ships. You can request a cancellation in the app; if the Seller hasn't shipped yet, they'll approve and we'll refund your original payment method.
  • After the Seller ships. Cancellation isn't possible; treat it as a return once the item arrives.
  • Sellers may cancel an order if the item can no longer be made or has been damaged before shipping. In that case we refund the Buyer in full.

Seller-specific policies

Sellers may offer more generous return terms than this policy (for example, 30-day buyer's-remorse returns). They may not offer less generous terms. If a Seller's listing conflicts with this policy, this policy wins.
