This Privacy Policy explains what information ShopMyNest ("ShopMyNest," "we," "us," "our"), a service operated by [LEGAL ENTITY], collects when you use shopmynest.com or the ShopMyNest mobile app (the "Service"), how we use it, and who we share it with.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information — name, email address, password (stored hashed), and, if you set one up, a profile photo.
  • Seller information — shop name, shop bio, ship-from address, package presets, payout details you provide to our payment processor, and any information you add to listings.
  • Buyer information — shipping address, billing address (via our payment processor), and order preferences.
  • Content you post — listings, photos, seller updates, moderated Blog submissions, messages, and reviews.
  • Support requests — anything you send to [CONTACT EMAIL] or through in-app support.

Information we collect automatically

  • Device and app information — device model, operating system, app version, and crash logs.
  • Usage information — pages viewed, features used, and search terms, together with a timestamp and coarse location derived from IP.
  • Cookies and similar technologies on the website — session cookies to keep you logged in, and analytics cookies to understand aggregate use. The mobile app uses secure token storage instead of browser cookies.

Information from other services

  • Payments — Stripe processes payments and seller payouts. Stripe returns confirmations, error codes, and payout status to us but does not share full card numbers with us.
  • Shipping — Shippo returns live shipping rates and label details when a Seller purchases a label.
  • Sign-in and integrations — if you use a third-party sign-in provider or connect another service, we receive the information that provider makes available to us.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect full payment-card numbers. Stripe collects and stores them directly.
  • The mobile app requests only the permissions it needs to work (photo library for listing photos, camera for photo capture, network). It does not collect your contacts, calendar, precise background location, or microphone audio.

How we use information

  • To operate the Service — accounts, listings, checkout, messaging, order tracking, shipping labels, and payouts.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud (with Stripe).
  • To send transactional emails and in-app notifications about orders, shipments, refunds, and account activity.
  • To review moderated Blog submissions before they appear publicly.
  • To communicate with you about product updates, features, and promotions (you can unsubscribe from marketing at any time; transactional messages continue).
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate abuse, fraud, and violations of our Terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our agreements.

Who we share information with

  • Other users of the Service. When you buy, the Seller gets your name and shipping address so they can ship your order. When you sell, Buyers see your shop name, listings, and any public profile information.
  • Service providers. Stripe (payments), Shippo (shipping and labels), our hosting/email/analytics vendors. They may only use information to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of ShopMyNest, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. If ShopMyNest is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user information may transfer with the business. We'll notify you before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

Data retention

We keep account information for as long as you have an account and for a reasonable time after closure to satisfy legal, tax, accounting, and dispute-resolution obligations. Order and payout records are retained for at least seven (7) years to meet US tax and financial-record requirements. Support communications are retained for a rolling period sufficient to service ongoing issues.

  • Push notifications. Manage them in your device settings.
